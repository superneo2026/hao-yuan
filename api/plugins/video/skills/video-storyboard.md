## Hook in 3 seconds
Open with a visual or verbal pattern break. State the promise of the video immediately.

## Keep a rhythm
30-60s total. Each shot 2-6s. Alternate wide / medium / close-up. Give every shot one job.

## Write for the ear
Narration should be spoken Chinese: short clauses, concrete verbs, no dense jargon. On-screen text is not a transcript.

## Deliverable
Call make_storyboard with title, theme, and shots: scene, duration, visual, narration. Include a production note that rendering is external.
