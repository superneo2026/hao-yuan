## Executive summary
Lead with the decision the reader must make, the recommended option, the expected outcome, and the required investment.

## Structure
Use: background, objectives, scope, market/context, solution, execution plan, milestones, budget, risks, KPIs, next actions.

## Quantify
Every important claim gets a number, a range, or a clear assumption. Separate facts from assumptions.

## Risks
Include probability, impact, mitigation, and owner. Do not list generic risks.

## Deliverable
Call make_plan with title, summary, 3-4 KPIs, and sections containing clean HTML: tables, callouts, timelines. Keep the document standalone and readable offline.
